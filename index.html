Hangover by Emmanuel Hernandez

Hangover puts your music, your day and your files in the MacBook notch.

Point at the notch and it opens. Move away and it closes. It's free, the code is open, and it keeps itself up to date.

Download for Mac Apple silicon, macOS 14 or later

What's in it

Music
Play, pause, skip and scrub, and pick the speaker, without leaving what you're doing.
Calendar
What's next today, in five looks. A heads-up in the notch before an event starts, and a Join button when it's a meeting.
To-dos
From Reminders, Notion or TickTick. Check one off right there.
Quick notes
Type a line and press Return. It goes to a Markdown file or to Apple Notes.
File tray
Drop a file on the notch and drag it back out when you need it. It can AirDrop or zip what's in it, and it keeps a clipboard history.
Timer
A focus timer with pomodoro rounds. The closed notch shows the time left.
Weather
Now, the next hours and the week.
Mirror
A camera check before a call, with frames, stickers and a ring light. It's also a photo booth.
Design kit
Palettes, a screen eyedropper, links and reference pictures for design work. Off until you turn it on.
Layouts
Start from a template, then press, hold and drag to move widgets and change their size.
Closed notch
Each side can show the date, your battery, the next event, the weather, a timer or your open to-dos.
Glow
A soft light around the notch when music plays or something needs you. You pick the colors, and it can pulse with the beat or throw lasers.
Accent color
Pick the color of every highlight, or let it follow the one your Mac is set to.
Swipe
Swipe up with two fingers to close it. It stays off until you turn it on.
Welcome tour
The first time you open it, a tour on the real notch sets everything up with you. It comes in three lengths, for however much time you have.

Use coding agents like Claude Code or Codex? Hangover shows what they're doing and lets you answer them from the notch. That part comes from Open Island, and you can switch it off.

Opening it

Hangover is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple. Open Hangover.zip from your Downloads, drag Hangover into your Applications folder and double-click it. macOS asks once if you want to open an app from the internet. Click Open.

There's no account and no analytics, and Hangover has no server of its own. All of the code is on GitHub. If something breaks, open an issue.
